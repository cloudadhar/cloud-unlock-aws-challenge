# Optional Challenge - Design The Fest Registration App

This is optional. Do it after the core labs.

## Scenario
Your fest website normally gets 50 visitors a day and 50,000 on registration day. Student data must stay in India.

## Tasks
1. Choose a Region and give four reasons (compliance, latency, service availability, cost).
2. Draw a simple diagram (paper, draw.io or Excalidraw) showing: users, CloudFront, a load balancer, two servers in **two Availability Zones**, and a database.
3. For each of the six pillars, write one sentence on how your design supports it.
4. List three problems from Day 1 your design solves.
5. Estimate the monthly cost in the AWS Pricing Calculator (calculator.aws). Add two small EC2 instances in Mumbai and one small database. You only build an estimate, so nothing is created and nothing is billed. Save the estimate and note the monthly total.

## Add To Your Submission
- `challenge/architecture.png`
- `challenge/pillars.md`
- `challenge/cost-estimate.md` (monthly total and one line on what costs the most)

You do not need to build it. We build real pieces in later weeks.
