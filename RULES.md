# Challenge Rules

## Safety (most important)
- Use your **own personal AWS account**. Never use someone else's or a company account.
- Never share or commit: root email/password, OTP codes, MFA QR code or secret key, access keys, secret keys, card or payment details, detailed billing details.
- Hide or blur your 12-digit AWS account ID, ARNs containing it, and email in screenshots.
- Use synthetic (fake) data only. No real personal data in any lab.
- Keep resources in the Mumbai Region (`ap-south-1`) unless the lab says otherwise.
- Set a budget alert before creating any resource. **Budgets alert, they do not stop spending.**
- Clean up every lab the same day. Cleanup is part of the challenge.

## How to submit
1. Fork this repo.
2. Create `week-XX/submissions/your-name/` (lowercase, hyphens, for example `week-01/submissions/asha-patil/`).
3. Add `README.md`, `notes.md`, and a `screenshots/` folder as shown in each week's `submission-format` file.
4. Open a pull request titled `Week XX - Your Name`.
5. An automatic check scans your files for keys and secrets. Fix anything it flags and push again.
6. Add your LinkedIn post link in your submission README.

## Minimum accepted submission (every week)
- `README.md` with what you learned and where you got stuck
- At least 2 safe screenshots
- One learning note
- One LinkedIn post link

Submit what you finished. An honest partial submission is better than silence.

## LinkedIn
- Write in your own words, 3-5 lines. Do not copy-paste the same post as others.
- Add one safe screenshot if useful.
- End every post with the tag line in that week's LinkedIn file.

## Deadlines
Submit by **Friday 11:59 PM IST** after the challenge weekend. Late submissions are welcome; mark them "late".

## Fair play
- Do your own labs. Learn from others, but do not copy someone else's screenshots.
- Be kind in discussions. Ask questions in the community instead of skipping a week.

## Recognition
Learners who complete the minimum submission for each week will be recognised by the AWS Student Builder Group (details announced in class).
